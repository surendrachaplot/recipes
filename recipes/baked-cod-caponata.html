Baked Cod with Caponata

14 May 2026

Serves 2. Caponata is a Sicilian sweet-sour eggplant dish — make extra, it keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets (~150g each), or any firm white fish
  • 1 tbsp olive oil + squeeze lemon
  • Black pepper and dried oregano

Caponata:

  • 1 large eggplant, cut into 2cm cubes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • Handful green olives, pitted and roughly chopped
  • Handful flat-leaf parsley

Method

  1. Caponata: Salt the eggplant cubes, leave 10 min, then pat dry. Fry in olive oil over high heat until golden on all sides. Remove. Same pan: onion and celery, 5 min. Tomatoes, vinegar, honey in. Return eggplant. Simmer 15 min. Stir in capers and olives. Taste — should be sweet and sour. Parsley to finish.
  2. Preheat oven to 190°C. Rub cod with olive oil, lemon, black pepper, and oregano. Bake 12–15 min until it just flakes.
  3. Spoon caponata onto plates. Cod on top.

Tip: Caponata improves overnight. Make it ahead and the fish is a 15-minute dinner.
