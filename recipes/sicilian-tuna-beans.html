Thursday — Sicilian White Beans with Tuna, Lemon & Chili

Serves 2. A 10-minute pantry dinner that punches way above its weight.

Ingredients

  • 1 × 400g can cannellini beans, drained (no-salt-added if available)
  • 1 × 150g can tuna in olive oil, drained (or in water)
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (the good stuff)
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced wafer-thin
  • Pinch chili flakes
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • Big handful flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • Handful cherry tomatoes, halved (optional)
  • Couple of handfuls of arugula (optional)
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Olive oil into a small pan over low heat. Garlic and chili flakes. Warm — not fry — for 2 min until the garlic just softens and smells sweet.
  2. Off the heat. Tip in the beans, stir gently to coat. Warm through.
  3. Into a bowl: beans, flaked tuna, lemon zest and juice, parsley, tomatoes if using.
  4. Toss. Black pepper. Skip the salt — taste first; the tuna and beans usually carry enough.
  5. Pile onto arugula or eat with a slice of whole-grain bread to mop the bowl.

Tip: Trick is good olive oil and not over-stirring — you want the tuna in chunks, not paste.

Heart-healthy weekday dinners — no shellfish, no yoghurt, no eggs.
