Wednesday — Miso-Glazed Salmon with Sesame Greens

06 May 2026

Serves 2. Twenty minutes start to finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets, skin-on (~150g each)
  • Glaze: 1 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp grated ginger, ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 heads bok choy, halved lengthwise (or 200g spinach)
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced thin
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Splash of low-sodium soy
  • Steamed brown rice, to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat broiler/grill to high. Whisk glaze. Pat salmon dry, brush glaze on top.
  2. Line a tray with foil. Salmon skin-down. Broil 6–8 min — glaze should bubble and char in spots; flesh should flake but stay just translucent in the center.
  3. Meanwhile: oil in a wide pan over medium-high. Garlic 20 seconds. Bok choy cut-side down, 2 min. Splash of soy + 2 tbsp water, lid on, 2 min more.
  4. Plate greens, salmon on top, sesame seeds. Rice if you want it.

Tip: Miso burns fast. If your broiler's enthusiastic, drop the salmon a rack lower and watch it like you owe it money.

Heart-healthy weekday dinners — no yoghurt, no eggs, no shellfish.
