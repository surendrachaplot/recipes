Spaghetti with Sardines, Capers & Chili

03 Jun 2026

Serves 2. Sardines are one of the highest omega-3 foods on earth, cheap as anything, and this Sicilian pasta is one of the most underrated weeknight dinners in existence.

Ingredients

  • 200g whole-wheat spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 × 120g cans sardines in olive oil, drained (keep 1 tbsp oil)
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ½–1 tsp chili flakes (to taste)
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • Handful green olives, pitted and roughly chopped (optional)
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Big handful flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp toasted breadcrumbs (whole-wheat, optional — sub for Parmesan, which you're skipping)

Method

  1. Cook pasta in a large pot of well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 100ml pasta water before draining.
  2. While pasta cooks: in a wide pan over low heat, warm olive oil and sardine oil together. Add garlic and chili flakes. Slow-cook 3 min — don't rush this, the garlic should be pale gold and sweet, not brown and bitter.
  3. Add sardines. Break them up gently with a wooden spoon — you want chunks, not mush. Capers and olives in. 1 min.
  4. Drained pasta into the pan. Toss. Splash in pasta water as needed to loosen. Lemon zest and juice over. Parsley in. Toss again.
  5. Plate. Toasted breadcrumbs over the top for crunch. Extra lemon, extra chili if you want it.

Tip: The breadcrumbs do the job Parmesan normally does — crunch, salt, savory depth. Don't skip them.
