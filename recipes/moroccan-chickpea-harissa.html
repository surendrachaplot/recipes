Tuesday — Moroccan Chickpea & Harissa Stew

05 May 2026

Serves 4 — leftovers reheat brilliantly. Make Tuesday for Wednesday's lunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • 1–2 tbsp harissa paste (start with 1)
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 × 400g cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed (no-salt-added if available)
  • 250ml low-sodium vegetable stock
  • ¼ preserved lemon, finely chopped (optional but worth it)
  • Big handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Olive oil into a deep pan over medium. Onion, soft and translucent, ~6 min.
  2. Garlic and dry spices in. Stir 30 seconds — toasted, not burnt.
  3. Harissa, tomatoes, chickpeas, stock. Bring to a simmer. Lid askew, 20 min, stirring now and then. Should thicken into something stew-like.
  4. Stir in preserved lemon. Taste before salting — preserved lemon brings its own. Off the heat.
  5. Pile parsley on top. Lemon wedge on the side. Eat with whole-grain couscous or just a hunk of good bread.

Tip: Day-two version is better than day one.
