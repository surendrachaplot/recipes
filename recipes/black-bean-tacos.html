Black Bean Tacos with Mango Avocado Salsa

13 May 2026

Serves 2–3. Fully plant-based. Ready in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 × 400g cans black beans, drained (no-salt-added)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Pinch chili flakes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 small corn or whole-wheat tortillas
  • 1 small red cabbage, shredded
  • Lime juice, to dress cabbage

Salsa: 1 ripe mango (diced), 1 avocado (diced), ½ red onion (finely diced), 1 jalapeño (finely diced), juice of 2 limes, big handful cilantro, pinch salt

Method

  1. Make the salsa first: toss mango, avocado, onion, jalapeño, lime, and cilantro together. Set aside.
  2. Dress shredded cabbage with lime juice and a pinch of salt. Leave to soften slightly while you do everything else.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high. Add beans and spices. Stir and cook 4–5 min until heated through and starting to crisp at the edges.
  4. Warm tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry pan.
  5. Build tacos: cabbage, beans, generous salsa on top.

Tip: Add the avocado to the salsa last so it doesn't go brown while you cook the beans.
