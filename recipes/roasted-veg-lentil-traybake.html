Roasted Vegetable & Lentil Traybake

04 Jun 2026

Serves 2–3. Everything on one tray. The lentils soak up the roasting juices. One of those recipes that's less than the sum of its parts until it isn't.

Ingredients

  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
  • 1 medium eggplant, cut into 3cm cubes
  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into 3cm chunks
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 garlic cloves, left whole in skin
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 × 400g can green or brown lentils, drained and rinsed
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 100ml low-sodium vegetable stock or water
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Big handful flat-leaf parsley
  • Whole-grain bread, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 210°C. Toss eggplant, pepper, zucchini, and onion with olive oil, paprika, and cumin. Spread across a large roasting dish. Tuck in the garlic cloves. Roast 20 min until softened and starting to colour.
  2. Pull the tray out. Squeeze the garlic out of its skin and mash into the vegetables. Add the lentils, tomatoes, and stock. Stir to combine. Return to oven for 20 min more — the tomatoes should thicken and the lentils absorb the liquid.
  3. Squeeze lemon over. Scatter parsley. Check seasoning.
  4. Bring the tray to the table. Eat with good bread to mop the juices.

Tip: Don't use canned red lentils here — they'll dissolve into mush. Green or brown lentils hold their shape through the roast.
