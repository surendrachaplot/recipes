Black Bean & Avocado Bowl with Lime-Cilantro Slaw

28 May 2026

Serves 2. Uses up the red cabbage, avocado, black beans, and cilantro from taco night. Deconstructed taco energy, no tortilla needed.

Ingredients

  • 1 × 400g can black beans, drained (or leftover from taco night)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Pinch chili flakes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • Brown rice or quinoa, to serve
  • Lime wedges

Lime-cilantro slaw: ¼ red cabbage finely shredded (leftover), 1 carrot julienned, juice of 2 limes, big handful cilantro, ½ red onion very thinly sliced, 1 jalapeño finely sliced, pinch salt

Quick tomato salsa: 200g cherry tomatoes (halved), 1 garlic clove (grated), splash lime juice, cilantro, salt

Method

  1. Make the slaw first: toss all slaw ingredients together. Leave 10 min — it softens and the flavours open up.
  2. Make the salsa: toss tomatoes with garlic, lime, and cilantro. Set aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high. Add beans and spices. Cook 4–5 min, stirring occasionally, until heated through and slightly crispy at the edges.
  4. Build bowls: rice or quinoa base, spiced beans, slaw piled alongside, avocado slices, salsa over the top.
  5. Squeeze a lime wedge over everything at the table.

Tip: Don't slice the avocado until the last moment. Everything else can sit, avocado cannot.
