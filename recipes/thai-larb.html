Friday — Thai Chicken Larb with Brown Rice

08 May 2026

Serves 2. Loud, sour, hot. Wake your mouth up.

Ingredients

  • 300g lean ground chicken (breast meat)
  • 2 tbsp jasmine or sticky rice (for toasted rice powder)
  • 2 shallots, sliced thin
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1–2 bird's eye chilies, finely sliced (calibrate to bravery)
  • Big handful fresh mint
  • Big handful cilantro
  • Dressing: 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tsp honey
  • Brown jasmine rice, to serve
  • Lettuce cups (optional, for wrapping)

Method

  1. Toasted rice powder first: dry-toast the rice in a small pan over medium until deep golden and nutty, 4–5 min. Cool, then grind in a mortar or blitz briefly. Texture of coarse sand.
  2. Same pan, no oil. Crumble in chicken over medium-high. Break it up small with a spoon. Cook 5–6 min until cooked through and starting to catch.
  3. Off the heat. Tip into a bowl. While warm: shallots, spring onions, chili, dressing, half the rice powder. Toss.
  4. Add the herbs last — torn, not chopped. Toss once.
  5. Pile over brown rice or into lettuce cups. Remaining rice powder on top.

Tip: The toasted rice powder is what makes it larb. Make extra and keep it in a jar — it's good on everything.
