Lemongrass Chicken & Rice Vermicelli Soup

26 May 2026

Serves 3–4. Uses up leftover lemongrass and vermicelli from the bún gà nướng night. This is essentially that bowl turned into a deeply aromatic soup.

Ingredients

  • 2 skinless chicken breasts (~300g)
  • 3 stalks lemongrass, bruised and cut into 4cm lengths
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, sliced into coins
  • 3 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1.2L low-sodium chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (low-sodium if available)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 200g rice vermicelli, dry
  • 2 heads baby bok choy, halved
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 bird's eye chili, thinly sliced
  • Big handful cilantro and fresh mint
  • Lime wedges, to serve
  • Bean sprouts (optional)

Method

  1. Combine stock, lemongrass, ginger, and garlic in a pot. Bring to a gentle simmer. Add chicken breasts whole. Poach over low heat, 15–18 min until cooked through. Don't boil — poaching keeps the chicken tender.
  2. Remove chicken. Strain the broth — discard the aromatics. Return broth to the pot. Season with fish sauce and honey. Taste — it should be fragrant, lightly salty, slightly sweet.
  3. Shred the chicken by hand into rough pieces.
  4. Soak vermicelli in just-boiled water 4–5 min. Drain. Drop bok choy into the hot broth for 1–2 min.
  5. Build bowls: vermicelli, bok choy, shredded chicken, spring onions, chili, herbs, sprouts. Ladle hot broth over. Squeeze of lime at the table.

Tip: The lemongrass fragrance is strongest right after straining. Serve immediately — it fades as it sits.
