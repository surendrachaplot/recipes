Ground Turkey & Farro Stuffed Peppers

02 Jun 2026

Serves 2–4 (makes 4 halves). Meal-preps brilliantly. Reheat in the oven at 180°C for 15 min.

Ingredients

  • 2 large red or yellow bell peppers, halved lengthwise, seeds removed
  • 300g lean ground turkey
  • 150g pearled farro, cooked (about 350g cooked weight)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • Handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • Black pepper
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Brush pepper halves with a little olive oil. Roast cut-side up 15 min while you make the filling.
  2. Oil in a pan over medium-high. Onion until soft, 5 min. Garlic in, 1 min. Turkey in — break it up and cook until browned, 6–7 min.
  3. Spices in. Stir 30 seconds. Add half the can of tomatoes (save the rest for a sauce or another night). Cook 3 min until thickened.
  4. Off the heat. Stir in cooked farro and most of the parsley. Taste and season.
  5. Spoon filling generously into roasted pepper halves, mounding it up. Return to oven 15 min until the tops start to catch.
  6. Remaining parsley over the top. Lemon wedge on the side.

Tip: Farro's nuttiness works better here than rice — don't substitute if you can help it.
