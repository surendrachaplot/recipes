Miso Ramen with Tofu, Bok Choy & Mushrooms

05 Jun 2026

Serves 2. This is not instant ramen. This is a 25-minute bowl that tastes like it took three hours.

Ingredients

Broth:

  • 1L low-sodium vegetable or chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp gochujang or chili paste (optional, for heat)

Bowl:

  • 200g ramen noodles (or soba as a substitute)
  • 200g firm tofu, cut into 1cm slabs
  • 150g shiitake or chestnut mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 heads baby bok choy, halved
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • Sheet of nori (dried seaweed), torn — optional

Method

  1. Press tofu briefly. Heat oil in a non-stick pan over high. Tofu slabs in — 2–3 min per side until golden. Set aside.
  2. Same pan: mushrooms over high heat, no stirring for 2 min until they colour. Season with a splash of soy. Set aside.
  3. Blanch bok choy in boiling water 1 min. Drain.
  4. For the broth: gently heat stock over medium. Whisk in miso (never boil miso — it kills the probiotics and dulls the flavour). Add soy, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and gochujang if using. Taste — it should be deeply savoury.
  5. Cook noodles per packet. Drain.
  6. Divide noodles between deep bowls. Ladle over hot broth. Arrange tofu, mushrooms, and bok choy. Spring onions, sesame seeds, and nori on top.

Tip: Never boil miso broth. Bring the stock to temperature, pull it off the heat, then whisk the miso in. The difference is real.
