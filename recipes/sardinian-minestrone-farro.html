Sardinian-Style Minestrone with Farro

20 May 2026

Serves 4–6. Farro gives this soup a nutty chew regular minestrone lacks. A pot of longevity food.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 1 × 400g can borlotti or cannellini beans, drained
  • 150g pearled farro (or spelt), rinsed
  • 1.5L low-sodium vegetable stock
  • 150g cavolo nero (Tuscan kale) or regular kale, stems removed, torn
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Good extra virgin olive oil, to drizzle
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Olive oil in a large pot over medium. Onion, carrot, celery — cook low and slow, 10 min, until truly soft.
  2. Garlic and rosemary in. 1 min. Tomatoes in. Cook 5 min.
  3. Beans, farro, and stock in. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer. Cook 25 min until farro is tender.
  4. Stir in kale. Cook 5 min more. Squeeze lemon in. Taste and season.
  5. Ladle into deep bowls. Drizzle of good olive oil, black pepper. That's it.

Tip: Farro absorbs a lot of stock overnight — add more water when reheating. The soup only gets better.
