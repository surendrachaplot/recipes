Spiced Lamb Kofta with Tabbouleh

21 May 2026

Serves 2–3. The kofta take 5 min to mix. The tabbouleh is the work, and it's worth it.

Ingredients

Kofta:

  • 350g lean ground lamb
  • 1 small onion, grated (squeeze out excess water)
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp chili flakes
  • Small handful flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Black pepper

Tabbouleh:

  • 100g bulgur wheat
  • Large bunch flat-leaf parsley (mostly leaf, some stem), very finely chopped
  • Small bunch fresh mint, very finely chopped
  • 4 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 3 medium ripe tomatoes, deseeded and finely diced
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Soak bulgur in just-boiled water for 15 min. Drain and squeeze dry in a clean cloth. Cool.
  2. Mix kofta ingredients. Shape into 8–10 oval patties around skewers or just as free-form patties. Chill 10 min if time allows.
  3. Combine all tabbouleh ingredients. Parsley should dominate — this is a herb salad, not a grain salad. Taste, season, toss.
  4. Grill or pan-fry kofta over high heat, 3–4 min per side until nicely charred and cooked through.
  5. Serve kofta on a bed of tabbouleh. A wedge of lemon.

Tip: Real tabbouleh is 70% herb, 30% everything else. Most people get this backwards.
