Turkish Red Lentil Soup (Mercimek Çorbası)

18 May 2026

Serves 4. Silky, warming, deeply satisfying. One of the great soups of the world.

Ingredients

  • 200g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, roughly chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 carrot, roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika (sweet)
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.2L low-sodium chicken or vegetable stock
  • Lemon wedges, to serve
  • Pinch dried mint + chili flakes + drizzle olive oil, to finish
  • Whole-wheat flatbread, to serve

Method

  1. Oil in a large pot over medium. Onion, garlic, carrot — soften 8 min. Spices and tomato paste in. Stir 1 min.
  2. Lentils and stock in. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 20–25 min until lentils are completely soft.
  3. Blend until silky smooth. Return to pot. Thin with water if needed. Season gently.
  4. Ladle into bowls. Drizzle olive oil, sprinkle mint and chili flakes, big squeeze of lemon. Serve with flatbread.

Tip: The lemon at the end is mandatory — it lifts the whole soup from good to brilliant.
