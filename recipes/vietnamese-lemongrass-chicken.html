Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken Bowl (bún gà nướng)

04 May 2026

Serves 2. Marinate ahead if you can — the bowl pays you back.

Ingredients

  • 2 skinless chicken breasts (~300g), sliced thin
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, white parts only, finely minced
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (low-sodium if available)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil + ½ tsp turmeric (optional, for color)
  • Black pepper
  • 200g rice vermicelli, dry
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • Big handfuls fresh mint and cilantro
  • 1 head butter or romaine lettuce, torn
  • Bean sprouts (optional)

Nuoc cham: 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp fish sauce, 2 tsp honey, 1 grated garlic clove, 1 minced bird's eye chili, 4 tbsp water

Method

  1. Mix lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, honey, oil, turmeric, and pepper. Toss with chicken. Marinate 20 min (or up to overnight).
  2. Soak vermicelli in just-boiled water 4–5 min until tender. Drain, rinse cold.
  3. Whisk nuoc cham. Should taste sour-salty-sweet-hot, in that order.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan or grill pan hot. Cook chicken 2–3 min per side until charred at the edges and just cooked through.
  5. Build bowls: noodles, lettuce, herbs, cucumber, carrot, sprouts, chicken on top. Pour over the nuoc cham at the table.

Tip: If you can find Thai basil, throw that in too. The fresh herbs aren't garnish — they're the dish.
