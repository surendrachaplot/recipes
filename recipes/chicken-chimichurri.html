Grilled Chicken Thighs with Chimichurri & Roasted Peppers

19 May 2026

Serves 2. Chimichurri is one of the great sauces — bright, herby, acidic. Make extra.

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless chicken breasts (~600g), lightly pounded to even thickness
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika, olive oil, black pepper — for the rub
  • 2 red bell peppers, halved and deseeded
  • Olive oil for peppers

Chimichurri: Large bunch flat-leaf parsley (stalks and all), 4 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp chili flakes, 80ml extra virgin olive oil, pinch salt

Method

  1. Make chimichurri: blitz everything together briefly — you want texture, not a purée. Or chop by hand for a chunkier version. Let it sit 20 min for the flavors to open up.
  2. Toss peppers with olive oil. Grill or roast at 220°C until softened and charred in spots, ~20 min.
  3. Rub chicken with paprika, olive oil, and pepper. Grill over medium-high: 5–6 min per side until cooked through (internal temp 74°C/165°F). Rest 5 min before slicing.
  4. Plate chicken and peppers. Spoon chimichurri generously over. Extra on the side.

Tip: Chimichurri keeps for a week in the fridge under a thin layer of oil. Use it on everything.
