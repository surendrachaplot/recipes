Miso-Glazed Cod with Sesame Bok Choy

03 Jun 2026

Serves 2. The miso glaze from the salmon night works on any fish. Cod holds it differently — firmer, meatier, less rich. Finish the miso jar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets (~150g each), or any firm white fish

Miso glaze: 1½ tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp grated ginger, ½ tsp toasted sesame oil, ½ tsp honey

  • 3 heads baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
  • 1 garlic clove, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • Splash of low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced (from the trout night bag)
  • Brown rice, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk glaze. Pat cod dry and brush generously on the top side. Marinate 15 min at room temperature while oven heats to 200°C.
  2. Line a tray with foil. Cod on, glaze-side up. Roast 12–14 min until the glaze caramelises and the fish flakes at the thickest point.
  3. Meanwhile: oil in a wide pan over medium-high. Garlic 20 seconds. Bok choy cut-side down, press lightly, 2 min. Splash of soy + 2 tbsp water, lid on, 2 min more.
  4. Plate: rice, bok choy, cod on top. Sesame seeds over the fish. Sliced radishes alongside for crunch and freshness.

Tip: The glaze is thick — it catches fast. Watch the last 2 minutes closely. Dark is good; black is not.
