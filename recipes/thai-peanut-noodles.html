Thai Peanut Noodles with Edamame & Shredded Carrot

29 May 2026

Serves 2. Served cold or at room temperature. This is Friday food — minimal cooking, maximum satisfaction.

Ingredients

  • 200g soba noodles or rice noodles
  • 150g frozen edamame, defrosted
  • 2 medium carrots, julienned or coarsely grated
  • ½ red cabbage, finely shredded (about 150g)
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • Big handful cilantro
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 bird's eye chili, thinly sliced (optional)

Peanut sauce: 3 tbsp natural peanut butter (no added sugar), 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tsp honey, 1 garlic clove (grated), 1 tsp grated ginger, 3–4 tbsp water to thin

Method

  1. Cook noodles per packet. Drain, rinse under cold water. Shake dry.
  2. Whisk peanut sauce. It will look seized initially — keep whisking, it comes together. Add water until it's a pourable dressing consistency.
  3. Combine noodles, edamame, carrot, cabbage, and spring onion in a large bowl. Pour sauce over and toss thoroughly.
  4. Plate up. Cilantro, sesame seeds, and chili on top. Extra lime wedge on the side.

Tip: Make extra sauce. It keeps 5 days in the fridge and improves everything it touches — grilled chicken, roasted veg, steamed greens.
