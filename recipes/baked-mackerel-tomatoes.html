Tuesday — Baked Mackerel with Roasted Tomatoes & Capers

Serves 2. Mackerel is one of the best heart foods on the planet — omega-3 through the roof.

Ingredients

  • 2 whole mackerel, gutted and cleaned (or 4 fillets)
  • 300g cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 lemon, half sliced into rounds, half for juicing
  • Fresh thyme or oregano sprigs
  • Black pepper
  • Handful flat-leaf parsley, to finish
  • Whole-grain bread, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C/390°F. Toss tomatoes, capers, and garlic with 1 tbsp olive oil. Spread in a roasting dish. Roast 15 min until blistered.
  2. Score the fish 3 times on each side. Stuff with lemon rounds and thyme. Nestle on top of the tomatoes. Drizzle remaining oil over fish.
  3. Roast 15–18 min until the flesh flakes away from the backbone easily.
  4. Squeeze lemon juice over everything. Scatter parsley. Eat with bread to mop the roasting juices.

Tip: Don't fear mackerel's boldness — the capers and tomatoes are exactly the right foil for it.

Heart-healthy weekday dinners — no shellfish, no yoghurt, no eggs.
