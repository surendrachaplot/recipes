Korean-Style Ground Turkey Bowl (no egg)

15 May 2026

Serves 2. Fast, satisfying, and punchy. This is bibimbap energy without the egg.

Ingredients

  • 300g lean ground turkey (or chicken)
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Brown rice, to serve

Toppings: 1 cucumber (thinly sliced, dressed with rice vinegar + sesame oil + pinch sugar), 1 carrot (julienned), 2 spring onions (sliced), 1 tsp sesame seeds, handful baby spinach or sautéed beansprouts

Method

  1. Mix soy, gochujang, sesame oil, honey, garlic, and ginger into a sauce.
  2. Heat a pan over medium-high, no oil. Add turkey, break up with a spoon. Cook 5–6 min until browned through.
  3. Pour sauce over turkey. Stir and cook 1–2 min more until glossy and fragrant. Pull off heat.
  4. Dress cucumber. Prep other toppings.
  5. Build bowls: rice at the base, turkey alongside arranged toppings. Sesame seeds over everything.

Tip: The gochujang is the whole point. Don't skip it — it's deep and fruity, not just hot.
