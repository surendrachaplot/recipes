Fish Sauce & Ginger Chicken with Bok Choy

04 Jun 2026

Serves 2. Uses up the fish sauce and ginger before they're forgotten at the back of the fridge. This Vietnamese-inspired braise is stupidly good for how simple it is.

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless chicken thighs (~500g), bone-in or boneless
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely grated
  • 1 bird's eye chili, sliced (or ½ tsp chili flakes)
  • 3 heads baby bok choy, halved
  • 3 spring onions, cut into 4cm lengths
  • Big handful cilantro
  • Jasmine or brown rice, to serve

Braising sauce: 2 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tsp honey, 100ml water or low-sodium chicken stock

Method

  1. Mix braising sauce and set aside. Pat chicken dry.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan or wok over high heat. Chicken thighs in — brown well on both sides, 3–4 min per side. Remove.
  3. Reduce heat to medium. Garlic, ginger, and chili in — 45 seconds, stirring. Don't let the garlic burn.
  4. Return chicken. Pour braising sauce over. Bring to a simmer, reduce heat, cover and cook 15 min until chicken is cooked through and sauce has reduced slightly.
  5. Nestle bok choy into the pan. Cover 3 min until just wilted. Spring onions in. Off the heat.
  6. Serve over rice. Cilantro over the top. Spoon the braising sauce generously.

Tip: The fish sauce is the seasoning — don't salt separately. Taste the sauce before adding anything else.
