Chicken Saag (Spinach Curry, No Cream)

28 May 2026

Serves 3–4. The restaurant version is mostly cream. This one is mostly spinach — better for you and, honestly, better tasting.

Ingredients

  • 500g skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric
  • ½ tsp chili powder (adjust to heat preference)
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 200g baby spinach (or 400g regular spinach, stems removed)
  • 100ml low-sodium chicken stock or water
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Small handful cilantro
  • Brown basmati rice or whole-wheat roti, to serve

Method

  1. Oil over medium-high. Chicken pieces in — brown on all sides, 4–5 min. Remove and set aside.
  2. Same pan over medium. Onion until golden, 8–10 min. Garlic and ginger in — 1 min. All dry spices in — 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
  3. Tomatoes in. Cook 5 min until the sauce thickens and oil starts to separate at the edges.
  4. Return chicken plus the stock. Simmer 10–12 min covered until chicken is cooked through.
  5. Add spinach in large handfuls, stir until wilted, 2–3 min. Squeeze lemon over. Taste and season. Scatter cilantro.

Tip: The sauce should look a little dry when you add the spinach — spinach throws out a lot of water. Don't panic and don't add extra liquid.
