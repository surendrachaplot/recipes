Tuna & White Bean Niçoise Salad (No Egg)

01 Jun 2026

Serves 2. The classic Niçoise, minus the egg, plus beans for extra heft. Better, honestly.

Ingredients

  • 2 × 120g cans tuna in olive oil, drained (keep 1 tbsp of the oil)
  • 1 × 400g can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 200g green beans, trimmed
  • 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • Handful black olives (Niçoise or Kalamata), pitted
  • Big handful flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • 2 Little Gem lettuces or 1 head Romaine, leaves separated

Dressing: 1 tbsp reserved tuna oil, 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, pinch chili flakes, black pepper

Method

  1. Blanch green beans in boiling water 3 min. Drain. Immediately plunge into cold water to keep them bright. Drain again.
  2. Soak red onion slices in cold water for 10 min to take the raw edge off. Drain.
  3. Whisk dressing. Taste — should be punchy.
  4. Arrange lettuce on a platter. Layer over beans, cherry tomatoes, green beans, onion, capers, and olives. Flake tuna in large chunks over the top — don't break it up too much. Scatter parsley.
  5. Drizzle dressing over at the table, not before. Serve immediately.

Tip: Good tuna (in olive oil, not water) and good olives make or break this. Don't dress too early or everything wilts.
