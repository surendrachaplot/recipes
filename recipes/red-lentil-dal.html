Monday — Red Lentil Dal with Wilted Spinach

11 May 2026

Serves 4. Freezes beautifully. A workhorse.

Ingredients

  • 250g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil or ghee
  • 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp chili powder (or to taste)
  • 1 × 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 900ml low-sodium vegetable stock
  • 100g baby spinach
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Small handful cilantro
  • Brown basmati rice or whole-wheat roti, to serve

Tadka: 1 tsp cumin seeds, ½ tsp mustard seeds, 1 dried red chili — bloomed in 1 tsp hot oil for 30 seconds

Method

  1. Oil in a large pot over medium. Onion until golden, 8–10 min. Add garlic and ginger, stir 1 min.
  2. Dry spices in. Stir 30 seconds. Tomatoes in. Cook 3 min until thickened and fragrant.
  3. Lentils and stock in. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook 20–25 min, stirring occasionally, until lentils are completely soft and collapsing.
  4. Stir in spinach — it wilts in 1 min. Squeeze lemon over. Taste and adjust salt.
  5. Make the tadka and drizzle over just before serving. Top with cilantro.

Tip: Dal thickens as it sits. Add a splash of water when reheating — it comes back perfectly.

Heart-healthy weekday dinners — no yoghurt, no eggs, no shellfish.
