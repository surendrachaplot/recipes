Silken Tofu & Bok Choy Stir-Fry with Ginger-Sesame Sauce

25 May 2026

Serves 2. Tofu is one of the most effective dietary interventions for LDL cholesterol. This one is actually delicious.

Ingredients

  • 400g firm tofu, pressed and cut into 2cm cubes
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch (for coating)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 heads baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely grated
  • 3 spring onions, cut into 3cm lengths
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Brown rice, to serve

Sauce: 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp cornstarch, 3 tbsp water, pinch chili flakes

Method

  1. Press tofu between paper towels for 10 min. Toss cubes in cornstarch. Mix sauce in a small bowl and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a wide non-stick pan over high heat. Add tofu in a single layer. Don't touch for 3 min until the bottom side is golden and crisp. Flip. Another 2 min. Remove and set aside.
  3. Same pan, still hot. Bok choy cut-side down. Char 2 min. Add garlic and ginger — 30 seconds. Spring onions in.
  4. Return tofu. Pour sauce over. Toss everything together — it will thicken almost immediately. 1 min max.
  5. Pile over brown rice. Sesame seeds over the top.

Tip: Press the tofu properly — wet tofu steams instead of crisping. Ten minutes with a heavy pan on top is all it takes.
