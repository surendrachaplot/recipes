Lemongrass Tofu Stir-Fry with Bok Choy

02 Jun 2026

Serves 2. Uses the remaining lemongrass stalks before they go. Lemongrass with tofu is underrated — the fragrance punches through in a way it can't with heavier proteins.

Ingredients

  • 400g firm tofu, pressed and cut into 2cm cubes
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, white parts only, very finely minced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 bird's eye chili, finely sliced
  • 3 heads baby bok choy, halved
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • Big handful cilantro
  • Jasmine rice, to serve

Sauce: 1 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp honey, juice of 1 lime, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Press tofu between paper towels 10 min. Toss in cornstarch to coat.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan or wok over high heat until smoking. Tofu in a single layer. Leave 3 min — don't touch. Flip when golden. 2 min more. Remove and set aside.
  3. Same pan, still hot. Lemongrass, garlic, ginger, and chili — stir-fry 1 min until deeply fragrant.
  4. Bok choy in. Toss 2 min until starting to wilt. Return tofu.
  5. Pour sauce over. Toss everything together 1 min until glossy. Off the heat — spring onions and cilantro in. Serve over rice.

Tip: Mince the lemongrass extremely fine — woody chunks are unpleasant to eat. Use the back of your knife to bruise it first, then mince.
