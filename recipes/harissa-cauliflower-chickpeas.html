Harissa-Spiced Cauliflower & Chickpea Couscous

26 May 2026

Serves 2–3. Roasting does something profound to cauliflower. Don't rush it — let it char.

Ingredients

  • 1 small head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1 × 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tbsp harissa paste
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 200g whole-wheat couscous
  • 250ml just-boiled low-sodium vegetable stock (for couscous)
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • Big handful flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • Small handful fresh mint, roughly chopped
  • 2 tbsp toasted flaked almonds (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C. Toss cauliflower and chickpeas with harissa, olive oil, and cumin. Spread in a single layer on a large baking tray — crowding kills the char.
  2. Roast 25–30 min, turning halfway, until cauliflower has dark edges and chickpeas are crispy in places.
  3. Meanwhile: couscous into a bowl, pour over hot stock, cover with a plate, leave 5 min. Fluff with a fork. Stir in lemon zest, a squeeze of lemon, and the herbs.
  4. Pile couscous into bowls. Top with roasted cauliflower and chickpeas. Extra lemon juice over. Almonds if using.

Tip: The chickpeas at the edges of the tray will be almost chip-like. Fight over them.
