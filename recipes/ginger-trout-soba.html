Ginger-Soy Baked Trout with Sesame Soba Noodles

22 May 2026

Serves 2. Soba noodles are 100% buckwheat if you can find them — more fiber, better flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 rainbow trout fillets (~150g each)
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 garlic clove (grated)
  • 150g soba noodles (100% buckwheat if possible)
  • 1 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 2–3 tbsp water to thin
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • ½ cucumber, halved and thinly sliced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Mix marinade. Brush over trout. Set aside 15 min while oven heats to 200°C.
  2. Bake trout on a lined tray 10–12 min until just cooked through and a little caramelized at the edges.
  3. Cook soba per packet — usually 4–5 min. Drain, rinse under cold water immediately (stops them going gluey).
  4. Whisk tahini dressing. Toss cold soba with dressing, radish, cucumber, and spring onion.
  5. Pile noodles in bowls. Lay trout on top. Sesame seeds over everything.

Tip: Rinse soba cold immediately after draining — non-negotiable, otherwise they clump into one sad mass.

Heart-healthy weekday dinners — no yoghurt, no eggs, no shellfish.
